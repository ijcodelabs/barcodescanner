## Data Collection and Use

The core functionality of the Barcode Scanner application is designed to work offline, storing all scanned data directly on your device.

### Information Collected with Your Permission

To provide features of Our Application, We may request, with Your prior permission:

- **Camera Access (android.permission.CAMERA)**: The Application requests access to your Device's camera. This permission is used exclusively for the purpose of scanning barcodes. We do not store, transmit, or save any images or video from your camera. Only the decoded data from the barcode is processed and stored locally on your device.

### Optional Data Synchronization

The Application includes an optional feature to synchronize your scanned data with a backend of your own choosing.

- **Internet Access (android.permission.INTERNET)**: The Application uses an internet connection solely to enable this optional data synchronization feature. This feature allows you to connect the app to a backend server and database that you provide, configure, and control.

- **User-Managed Data**: If you choose to use the sync feature, the barcode data saved on your device will be transmitted to the backend server and database that you have specified in the app's settings. We, the developers of Barcode Scanner, do not provide the backend service, and we do not have access to, collect, or store this data on our servers. The responsibility for providing, managing, and securing the backend infrastructure lies entirely with you, the user.

## Security of Your Data

The security of Your data is a shared responsibility that depends on where the data is stored.

- **On Your Device**: All scanned data and configuration settings (such as the URL to your backend) are stored locally on your device. We recommend you use your device's security features (like screen locks and encryption) to protect this data.

- **On Your Server**: Since you provide and manage the backend server and database for the synchronization feature, you are solely responsible for the security of your data on that server. This includes securing your database, managing access, implementing encryption (like HTTPS), and ensuring its overall integrity. We cannot be held responsible for any data breaches that occur on your private backend infrastructure.
