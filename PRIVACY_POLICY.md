# Privacy Policy for Packt

**Last Updated:** January 2, 2026

## Introduction

Packt ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application for organizing and participating in group rallies and rides.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By using Packt, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

## Information We Collect

### Personal Information

When you create an account with Packt, we collect:

- **Email Address:** Used for account creation, authentication, and important communications
- **Username and Display Name:** Your chosen username and display name for identification within the app
- **Profile Information:** Optional profile image or avatar you choose to upload
- **Account Creation Date:** Timestamp of when you registered

### Location Data

Packt collects and processes location data, which is essential for the core functionality of coordinating group rallies:

- **Real-time GPS Location:** When you participate in a rally and enable location sharing, we collect your precise location (latitude, longitude)
- **Location Metadata:** Speed, bearing (direction), and GPS accuracy
- **Route History:** GPS points tracked during rallies you participate in
- **Rally Locations:** Start and end locations of rallies you create or join

**Important:** Location sharing is disabled by default and requires your explicit consent. You can enable or disable location sharing at any time in your app settings.

### Rally and Activity Data

- **Rally Information:** Details about rallies you create or join, including descriptions, dates, times, and participant lists
- **Rally Statistics:** Distance traveled, average speed, maximum speed, and duration of rallies
- **Participation History:** Records of rallies you've joined or created
- **Route Data:** GPS tracks and routes from your rally participation

### Usage and Device Information

- **Network Information:** Network connectivity status to ensure proper app functionality
- **Notification Preferences:** Your choices regarding push notifications
- **App Usage Data:** Information about how you interact with the app
- **Cached Map Data:** OpenStreetMap tiles temporarily stored on your device for offline viewing

## How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

### Core App Functionality

- **Account Management:** Creating and maintaining your user account
- **Rally Coordination:** Enabling you to create, join, and participate in group rallies
- **Real-time Location Sharing:** Displaying your location to other rally participants when you've enabled location sharing during active rallies
- **Route Tracking:** Recording and displaying rally routes and statistics
- **Communication:** Facilitating chat and notifications between rally participants

### Service Improvement

- **Performance Optimization:** Improving app performance and user experience
- **Feature Development:** Understanding usage patterns to develop new features
- **Technical Support:** Providing customer support and troubleshooting

### Notifications

- **Rally Notifications:** Alerting you about rally start times, new participants, and rally updates (when enabled)
- **Chat Notifications:** Notifying you of new messages from other participants (when enabled)
- **System Notifications:** Important app updates or account-related information

## Third-Party Services

Packt uses the following third-party services that may collect and process your data:

### Firebase (Google)

- **Firebase Authentication:** For secure user account management
- **Firebase Firestore:** For storing user profiles, rally data, and app content
- **Firebase Cloud Messaging:** For push notifications
- **Firebase Storage:** For storing profile images and rally-related files

Firebase is subject to Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

### Google Services

- **Google Play Services Location:** For accurate GPS location tracking
- **Google Places API:** For location search and address autocomplete

### OpenStreetMap

- **osmdroid:** For displaying maps. Map tiles are cached locally on your device
- OpenStreetMap Privacy Policy: https://wiki.osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy

### Weather Data Provider

- Weather information displayed for rallies is fetched from a third-party weather API

## Data Sharing and Disclosure

### Within the App

- **Rally Participants:** When you join a rally and enable location sharing, your real-time location is visible to other participants in that rally
- **Public Rallies:** Information about public rallies you create is visible to all app users
- **Private Rallies:** Information about private rallies is only visible to invited participants
- **Profile Information:** Your username, display name, and profile image are visible to other app users

### We Do NOT Sell Your Data

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

### Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required to do so by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities (e.g., court orders, government agencies).

### Business Transfers

In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction.

## Data Retention

- **Active Accounts:** We retain your data as long as your account is active
- **Deleted Accounts:** When you delete your account, we will delete your personal information within 30 days, except where we are required to retain it for legal purposes
- **Rally History:** Rally data may be retained for historical and statistical purposes in anonymized form

## Your Rights and Choices

### Location Control

- **Enable/Disable Location Sharing:** You can turn location sharing on or off at any time in your app settings
- **Active Rally Only:** Location is only tracked when you actively participate in a rally

### Notification Preferences

You can control notification settings for:
- Rally start notifications
- New participant notifications
- Chat notifications
- Rally begin notifications

### Account Management

- **Access Your Data:** You can view your account information and rally history within the app
- **Update Your Information:** You can edit your profile, username, and display name
- **Delete Your Account:** You can request account deletion by contacting us

### Device Permissions

You can revoke the following permissions at any time through your device settings:
- Location access (will limit rally participation features)
- Notification permissions
- Storage access (for map caching)

## Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your information:

- **Encryption:** Data transmitted between your device and our servers is encrypted using industry-standard protocols
- **Firebase Security:** We utilize Firebase's security rules to control data access
- **Authentication:** Secure email/password authentication with Firebase Authentication
- **Limited Access:** Only authenticated users can access rally and user data

However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## Children's Privacy

Packt is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected information from a child under 13, please contact us immediately.

## Location-Based Services

### Foreground Location Service

When you actively participate in a rally, Packt uses a foreground service to track your location. This is indicated by a persistent notification showing that location tracking is active.

### Background Location Processing

Location updates are processed in the background only when you are actively participating in a rally. Background processing is used to sync your location with other rally participants.

### Map Caching

Map tiles from OpenStreetMap are cached on your device for improved performance and offline viewing.

## International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed on servers located outside your country of residence. By using Packt, you consent to the transfer of your information to countries that may have different data protection laws than your country.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

- Posting the new Privacy Policy in the app
- Updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy
- Sending you a notification (for significant changes)

Your continued use of the app after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.

- **Right to Access:** Request copies of your personal data
- **Right to Rectification:** Request correction of inaccurate data
- **Right to Erasure:** Request deletion of your data
- **Right to Restrict Processing:** Request limitation of data processing
- **Right to Data Portability:** Request transfer of your data
- **Right to Object:** Object to certain types of processing
- **Right to Withdraw Consent:** Withdraw consent for location tracking and other optional features
